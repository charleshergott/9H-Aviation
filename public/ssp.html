Tier 1

Bank Financing (Primary Exit) Pending

🏦 If DSCR > 1.4x in Years 1-2
A Singapore or Hong Kong bank (Bangkok Bank, CIMB, DBS, UOB) pre-commits to a $6-7M acquisition facility at Year 4, contingent on SSP achieving DSCR > 1.4x.

How It Works:

Now - Week 4
Pre-commitment Letter: Bank issues conditional facility letter for $6-7M acquisition financing at ~5.5-6.5% interest
Year 1-2
Proof of Concept: SSP operates aircraft, demonstrates DSCR > 1.4x with actual revenue & expenses
Year 4
Facility Draws: Bank funds acquisition ($5.67M for 3 AC). Halo gets paid in full. SSP refinances with bank at 5.5-6.5%.

Why Banks Accept This:

DSCR > 1.4x = proven operator with real revenue. Aircraft is strong collateral (moveable, sellable). Seaplane operations in Southeast Asia are growing sector.
