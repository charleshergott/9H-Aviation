Aero Assets Asia Pte. Ltd., Seaplane Asia's joint venture focused on aircraft asset management and trading, signed a momentous aircraft purchase agreement for twelve (12) Cessna Grand Caravan EX Amphibian aircraft with Hainan Haizhi General Aviation, a subsidiary of Northern-Heli Aviation Group China.

This landmark deal represents one of the largest single-tranche commitments for the renowned Cessna Grand Caravan EX Amphibian in the Asia-Pacific region to date. The aircraft are equipped with Wipline 8750 amphibious floats, offering unparalleled connectivity across remote island resorts, coastal cities, and waterways.

"This landmark purchase of twelve Cessna Grand Caravan EX Amphibians is a watershed moment, not just for Aero Assets Asia, but for the entire regional aviation sector."
— Dennis Keller, CEO of Seaplane Asia & Director of Aero Assets Asia
"The selection of the Cessna Grand Caravan EX Amphibian was a rigorous technical decision, with a focus on our existing and growing operation with Siam Seaplane in Thailand, Santai Seaplane in Indonesia, Samra Seaplane in Cambodia and more to come."
— Fabrice Mouton, CTO of Seaplane Asia & Director of Aero Assets Asia

Why Phased Acquisition Wins

For SSP
✓ Acquire only as pipeline/revenue justify (no $21M balloon payment)

For Halo
✓ Lease income continues on remaining aircraft (5 AC stay leased until Year 7)
✓ Acquisition locked to DSCR milestones (no leakage risk)
✓ Steadier cash: Lease revenue Y1-7, exit payments Y3-7

Total Halo return (Lease + Acquisition): $16.5M lease revenue (Y1-7 rolling) + $20.14M acquisition proceeds = ~$36.6M gross over 7 years. Same IRR profile as lump acquisition, but with lower concentration risk.
